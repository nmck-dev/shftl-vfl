The 2026 season will be broken down into multiple play periods. At the beginning of each play period, your squad will be reset and your budget refilled to: 50 VP.

    Play Periods:
  • Kickoff
  • Masters (Santiago)
  • Split 1
  • Masters (London)
  • Split 2
  • Champions (Shanghai)

During Kickoff, Split 1, and Split 2 the league will be limited to America's teams and players.

During the Masters and Champions tournaments, the league will include all participating teams and players.

You will begin each Play Period with an empty roster and a budget of 50 VP to purchase your active roster. During Split 1 and Split 2 you will have a separate budget of 20 VP to purchase bench players.


    Active Squad Size:
  • 1 Duelist
  • 1 Initiator
  • 1 Controller
  • 1 Sentinel
  • 2 Wildcards

    Bench Squad Size:
  • 3 Wildcards

After selecting your team, you can buy and sell players in the transfer market.

Unlimited transfers can be made at no cost until the first deadline of each Play Period.

All changes to your team (starting squad, transfers, bench changes) must be made by the Game-week deadline in order to take effect.

Tournament deadlines will be the last day before matches start for each stage. (Swiss stage, Play-offs, Finals Weekend)

Regular season deadlines will be the last day before matches the weeks games begin.


Note: Players will only score their highest 2 scores in each Game-week. If they happen to play more than 2 matches in a single Game-week, then the lowest score will be dropped.


    Kills:
  • -3 points for 0 kills in a map
  • -1 points for 1-4 kills in a map
  • +1 point for 10 kills in a map (additional +1 point for every 5 additional kills)

    Multi-Kills:
  • +1 for a 4k in a round
  • +3 for a 5k+ in a round

    Map Wins:
  • +1 for any map win
  • +2 bonus for winning Best of 3 in 2 maps
  • +5 for 13-0 map win
  • -5 for 0-13 map loss
  • +2 for winning map by 10+ rounds
  • -2 for losing map by 10+ rounds
  • +1 for winning map by 5-9 rounds
  • -1 for losing map by 5-9 rounds

    Series Win:
  • +2 for 2-0 series win
  • +4 for 3-0 series win
  • +1 for 3-1 series win

    Bonus Points:
  • +3 for highest average VLR rating across match
  • +2 for second highest VLR rating across match
  • +1 for third highest VLR rating across match

- Do I have to use my entire budget?

No. You can spend anything under the budget limit and can then use the extra funds later in the transfer market.

- Can I change my squad during a Game-week?

No, squad changes are locked once a Game-week begins. You can only make changes during the gap between Game-weeks.

- What happens if a player gets benched/subbed?

Automatic substitutions will happen in the case of injury or non permanent stand-ins. If a player in your squad is permanently removed from your roster: the slot will become empty, the VP you spent on that player will be refunded and 1 transfer credit will be added to your account.
